IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Privacy notice

This website is a school information and admissions-enquiry starter. The admissions enquiry form prepares an email in the visitor’s email application. The visitor must review and send that email themselves. This website does not currently store form submissions in a database.

Information not to submit

Until the school approves a secure application process, do not send identity numbers, medical records, copies of certificates or other sensitive learner information through this demo website.

Before publication

The school should review and approve this notice and establish a process aligned with applicable South African privacy requirements, including POPIA. The school should confirm who receives, stores, accesses, retains and deletes personal information.
