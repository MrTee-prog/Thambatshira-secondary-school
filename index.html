Welcome to Thambatshira Secondary School.

It is my pleasure to welcome learners, parents, guardians and visitors to our school website. At Thambatshira Secondary School, we believe that quality education is built through commitment, discipline, respect, hard work and partnership between the school, learners and families.

As a school community, we encourage every learner to take responsibility for their learning, make the most of every opportunity and strive towards their goals. We value the contribution of our teachers, parents and guardians in supporting learners throughout their educational journey.

May this website help you stay connected with our school, access important information and celebrate the progress of our learners. Let us continue working together in the pursuit of excellence.

We strive for quality education.

About our school

A place to learn, grow and thrive.

Thambatshira Secondary School is based in Tshisahulu Village, Shayandima, in the Vhembe East education district of Limpopo Province, South Africa. The school serves learners in Grades 8 to 12.

Our motto, “We Strive for Quality Education,” reflects our commitment to learning, personal growth and preparing learners for the opportunities and responsibilities ahead.

Admissions & applications

Parents and guardians can use the enquiry form to contact the school about admission requirements and the application process.

Before you apply

Submitting an online enquiry does not confirm admission or replace any official Department of Education or school application documents.

  • Contact the school to confirm available places and application dates.
  • Ask which official forms and supporting documents are required.
  • Confirm whether certified copies, transfer documents or previous school reports are needed.
  • Keep a copy of every form you submit.

Need help? Email the school office at thambatshira.admissions@gmail.com.

Privacy first: please do not enter ID numbers, medical information or upload documents on this enquiry form.
