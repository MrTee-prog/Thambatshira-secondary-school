PREVIEW IN VS CODE
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder in VS Code.
3. Open index.html using Live Server, or double-click index.html to open it in a browser.

INSTALL AS A PHONE APP
Publish the website over HTTPS first. On iPhone, open the published website in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and choose Install app / Add to Home screen when available. The service worker caches the app shell for basic offline access after first visit.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS / BEFORE PUBLISHING
- The admissions enquiry form opens a prefilled email draft. The visitor must review and press Send. It does not automatically submit or store an application.
- The enquiry form is not the official learner application form.
- The general blank parent-consent template is not an official school policy form. The supplied camp consent form remains the separate school-provided document.
- Do not collect identity numbers, medical records, certificates or sensitive learner data until the school approves a secure process.
- The privacy notice is a draft and must be reviewed by the school with its POPIA responsibilities in mind.
- Confirm school details, dates, admissions requirements and notices before publication.
- Obtain appropriate permission before publishing identifiable learner or staff photographs.

CONSENT FORM UPDATE
